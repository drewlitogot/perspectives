What you'll practice
  • Notice how the same social setting can affect attention and comfort differently for two people.
  • Separate what someone did from a guess about their feelings or intentions.
  • Consider both people’s perspectives when a conversation changes.
  • Practice a clear question or request that respects the other person’s choices.

Two People, One Evening

Two people share a conversation at a busy café. Discover what each notices, try a respectful response, and see what can change when they ask rather than assume.
