If you started here: key terms

From the glossary, word for word.

Catfishing
Presenting a false or misleading identity online. Someone may use false photos or personal details to mislead people about who they are. See chapter 47.
Romance scam
A form of fraud in which someone uses the appearance of romantic interest or a relationship to obtain money, valuable information, or other benefits dishonestly. See chapter 47.
Sextortion
Threatening to expose someone’s intimate images or other sexual material unless they meet a demand. It is a form of exploitation, not a normal relationship disagreement. See chapter 47.
