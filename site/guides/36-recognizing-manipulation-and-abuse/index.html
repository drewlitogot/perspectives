If you started here: key terms

From the glossary, word for word.

Coercive control
An ongoing pattern of behavior that restricts someone’s freedom, choices, or independence in a relationship. It can involve fear, pressure, or isolation. See chapter 36.
Gaslighting
A pattern of undermining someone’s trust in their own memory, perceptions, or judgment. An ordinary disagreement about what happened is not automatically gaslighting. See chapter 36.
Love bombing
Intense attention or affection that becomes concerning when it is tied to pressure, control, or demands. Affection alone is not evidence of manipulation; the surrounding behavior matters. See chapter 36.
