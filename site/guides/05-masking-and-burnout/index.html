If you started here: key terms

From the glossary, word for word.

Autistic burnout
A pattern of prolonged exhaustion, reduced ability to manage everyday tasks, and lower tolerance for demands or sensory input. It can occur when a person’s needs and available energy have been strained over time. See chapter 5.
Masking
Hiding or suppressing your natural traits to fit in. See chapter 5.
Unmasking
Allowing more of your usual behavior or reducing efforts to hide your traits. You can decide when and with whom it feels appropriate to unmask. See chapter 5.
