If you need help now

In the US, if you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. For a mental health or suicidal crisis, call or text 988 (the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or chat at 988lifeline.org.

The book's crisis resources chapter lists more services, including help after sexual assault and abuse.

For ongoing help, see Therapy and support.
