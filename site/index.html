Relationships make more sense when you can see more than one perspective. A free, practical guide to dating, communication, boundaries and healthy relationships, written for people who experience the world differently, and for the people who date them.

Who it's for

Adults of any age who are dating from scratch, or who feel like beginners: people who never learned the unwritten rules, and people whose brains work differently. Many readers are autistic, ADHD or both, or carry childhood or relationship trauma, with or without a diagnosis.

It's also for anyone dating someone whose brain works differently from theirs, so both people can understand each other.

How it works

Each chapter explains one part of dating plainly, with sources. Boxes add the view from different genders and neurotypes, and for partners. Read in order or skip to what you need.

Consent comes first throughout. Nothing here teaches anyone to pressure, trick or wear someone down.
