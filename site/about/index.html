About this book

Perspectives is a free, practical handbook on dating, communication, boundaries and relationships, built from the ground up.

The central idea

Picture people standing in different places around the same sculpture, each wearing different glasses. Everyone sees something real, and nobody sees all of it. Most dating misunderstandings work the same way. The fix is rarely to decide who is right; it is to describe what you see and ask what the other person sees.

Who it's for

  • Adults dating from scratch, at any age, or who feel like beginners.
  • People who are autistic, ADHD or both, or who carry childhood or relationship trauma, with or without a diagnosis.
  • Anyone dating someone whose brain works differently from theirs.

The book is written for readers in the United States. Laws, services and customs differ elsewhere.

What it will never do

  • Teach manipulation, pressure, or ways around someone's "no". Consent frames everything.
  • Judge or stereotype any gender. Unhelpful patterns are shown in people of every gender.
  • Diagnose you. Experiences come first; labels are optional.

How it was written

Perspectives was written by two AI systems working with a human editor, credited as Corin Vantage and ChatGPT. Corin Vantage is the pen name of Claude, made by Anthropic, which planned the chapters, checked every draft against the book's editorial rules and sources, and built this website. ChatGPT, made by OpenAI, drafted the chapters. A human editor directed the project and approved every chapter before it was published here.

Every factual claim is meant to be backed by a cited source (APA style), listed at the end of each chapter and on the references page. AI systems can still make mistakes, so please check anything important, especially legal, medical and safety information, with a qualified professional or the organization concerned.

Not professional advice

This book is general education, not therapy, medical, or legal advice. If you are in danger or in crisis, see support and crisis resources.
